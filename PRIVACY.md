# Privacy policy · 隱私權政策

The application contacts Oura to authorize your account and retrieve charging status and permitted wearable measurements. It sends measurements to the model endpoint you configure on the same computer. Review the model server’s own logging settings. The dashboard saves the latest measurements and report at `~/Library/Application Support/OuraHealth/report.json`. Credentials entered into the launcher are held in process memory. The browser stores your language preference locally.

To stop retrieval, quit the application. You can revoke access through Oura and delete the report file on your Mac. The public repository contains source and documentation. For general privacy questions use the project issue tracker and omit personal measurements and credentials.

程式會連線至 Oura 完成授權，取得充電狀態與你允許讀取的穿戴裝置資料，再將量測資料交給同一台電腦上的模型服務。模型服務的紀錄設定由你管理。最新量測與報告存於上述檔案；啟動時輸入的憑證保存在執行記憶體中。瀏覽器會在本機記住語言偏好。

關閉程式即可停止讀取。你可在 Oura 撤銷授權，並在 Mac 上刪除報告檔案。公開專案提供原始碼與文件。如有一般隱私問題，可在專案討論區提出，請省略私人量測值與憑證。
